## Design Context

### Users
Friends on phones exchanging candid messages, with creators sharing private inbox links.

### Brand Personality
Friendly, warm, familiar; Haitian Creole first.

### Aesthetic Direction
"Vit" (glass): light, slick, modern, mobile-first. Cool off-white page, frosted-glass cards, one vivid Haitian blue (#2447ff) for actions, and a slow drifting sun-yellow glow behind the glass. Geist and Geist Mono, self-hosted in /public/fonts. Bottom sheets on phones, spring press feedback, anonymous carnival-mask avatars on messages. No italics, no serif accents, no emoji as icons. The share image reads its colors and fonts from the --share-* CSS variables.

### Design Principles
- Keep writing and sharing effortless.
- Keep private inbox access clear and safe.
- Respect small screens and readable contrast.
